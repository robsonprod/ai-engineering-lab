# Senior AI Engineer — LLM Platform (fintech, remote)

We are building the LLM platform behind customer support and internal tools of a digital bank.

## Requirements
- 5+ years of back-end development (Java, Kotlin or Python)
- Production experience with LLM applications: RAG, tool calling, structured outputs
- LangChain or LangGraph
- Evaluation of LLM systems (offline evals, regression tests for prompts)
- AWS (Lambda, ECS or EKS) and infrastructure as code
- Event-driven systems with Kafka

## Nice to have
- Vector databases (pgvector, OpenSearch)
- Observability for LLM apps (tracing, cost per request)
- Experience in regulated industries (banking, payments)
